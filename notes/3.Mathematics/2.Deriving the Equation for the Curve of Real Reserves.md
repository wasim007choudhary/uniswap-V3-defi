# Deriving the Equation for the Curve of Real Reserves

# Part 1

This lesson is one of the most important mathematical lessons in Uniswap V3.

At first glance, the equations look intimidating.

However, the actual objective of this lesson is surprisingly simple.

It answers one question:

> **How do we move from the Virtual Constant Product Curve to the Real Reserve Curve?**

Do **not** try to memorize the final equation.

Instead, understand **why** every step exists.

---

# Why Do We Need Another Equation?

Until now, we've been using the familiar Constant Product Equation:

```text
X × Y = L²
```

Question:

**What does this equation actually represent?**

Does it represent the actual amount of Token X and Token Y inside our Position?

**No.**

It represents the **Virtual Reserve Curve.**

---

# Didn't We Already Derive X and Y?

Yes.

Previously we derived:

```text
X = L / √P
```

and

```text
Y = L√P
```

These equations describe points on the **Virtual Constant Product Curve.**

---

# Why Isn't The Virtual Curve Enough?

Remember how concentrated liquidity works.

Unlike Uniswap V2,

our liquidity does **not** exist across every possible price.

Instead,

our Position only exists between:

```text
Pa

↓

Pb
```

Outside this range,

our liquidity is inactive.

Therefore,

the infinite Constant Product Curve cannot completely describe our Position.

We need another curve that represents **only the liquidity inside our chosen range.**

That curve is called the:

```text
Real Reserve Curve
```

---

Your house doesn't care about the rest of the road.

# Splitting The Reserves

The lesson now splits each reserve into two parts.

Instead of writing:

```text
X
```

it writes:

```text
X = Xr + Xv
```

Similarly,

instead of:

```text
Y
```

it writes:

```text
Y = Yr + Yv
```

Where:

- `Xr` = Real Token X
- `Xv` = Virtual Token X
- `Yr` = Real Token Y
- `Yv` = Virtual Token Y

# Substitute Into The Constant Product Equation

We already know:

```text
X × Y = L²
```

Now replace:

```text
X = Xr + Xv
```


```text

×

(Yr + Yv)

=

L²
```

Nothing magical happened.

We simply substituted the new expressions.

# Key Takeaways

- The Constant Product Equation:
  ```text
  X × Y = L²
  ```
  represents the **Virtual Reserve Curve**.
- A Uniswap V3 Position only exists between:
  ```text
  Pa

  ↓

  Pb
  ```
- Therefore, the Virtual Curve must be translated to obtain the Real Reserve Curve.
- The reserves are split into:
  ```text
  X = Xr + Xv

  Y = Yr + Yv
  ```
- Substituting these gives:
  ```text
  (Xr + Xv)(Yr + Yv) = L²
  ```
- At the upper boundary (`Pb`):
  ```text
  Xr = 0
  ```
  leading to:
  ```text
  Xv = L / √Pb
  ```
- At the lower boundary (`Pa`):
  ```text
  Yr = 0
  ```
  leading to:
  ```text
  Yv = L√Pa
  ```
- The Virtual Curve is shifted:
  - Left by:
    ```text
    L / √Pb
    ```
  - Down by:
    ```text
    L√Pa
    ```
- The Virtual Curve **never changes shape**. Only its position changes.
- This lesson derives the translation amounts only.
- The next lesson substitutes them into the translated equation to obtain the complete Real Reserve Curve equation.
