# Updating Variables Depending on the Swap Type

After calculating

- `amountIn`
- `amountOut`
- `fee`

the algorithm updates its local variables.

Interestingly,

this update is **different** depending on whether the swap is:

- Exact Input
- Exact Output

Let's understand why.

Remember




# Final Key Takeaways

- A swap does **not** happen in one giant calculation.
- The pool first gathers three important inputs:
  - `zeroForOne`
  - `amountSpecified`
  - `sqrtPriceLimit`
- The sign of `amountSpecified` determines whether the swap is **Exact Input** or **Exact Output**.
- The swap starts from the **current price (current tick)**.
- The pool repeatedly processes small portions of the swap inside a **while loop**.
- Every iteration:
  - Finds the next initialized tick.
  - Calculates `sqrtPriceNext`.
  - Computes `amountIn`, `amountOut`, and `fee`.
  - Updates the current price.
  - Updates the remaining swap amount.
- The loop stops when:
  - there is nothing left to swap, or
  - the price reaches the specified `sqrtPriceLimit`.
- After the loop, the pool updates its state, computes the final token deltas, executes the swap callback, receives the input tokens, verifies its balances, and completes the swap.
