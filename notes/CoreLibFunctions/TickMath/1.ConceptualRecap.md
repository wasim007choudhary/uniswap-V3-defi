# 🧠 TickMath — Conceptual Recap

Think of `TickMath` as a **conversion machine**:

```text
                 TickMath
                    │
        ┌───────────┴───────────┐
        ↓                       ↓
      TICK                  SQRT PRICE
        │                       │
        │                       │
        └────── conversion ────┘
```

It has two jobs:

```text
getSqrtRatioAtTick()
        │
        ↓
   tick → sqrtPriceX96
```

and

```text
getTickAtSqrtRatio()
        │
        ↓
   sqrtPriceX96 → tick
```

---

## 1. 📍 What is a Tick?

A tick is basically a **discrete price position**.

Uniswap V3 spaces prices using:

### 📐 Formula — Price represented by a tick

\[
P = 1.0001^{tick}
\]

So:

```text
tick increases
     ↓
price increases

tick decreases
     ↓
price decreases
```

Example conceptually:

```text
tick -2    tick -1    tick 0    tick 1    tick 2
   ↓          ↓         ↓         ↓         ↓
 lower      lower       1       higher    higher
 price      price               price     price
```

---

# 2. 🔢 Why `1.0001`?

Each tick represents approximately a **0.01% price step**.

So:

```text
tick 0
   ↓
price = 1

tick 1
   ↓
price = 1.0001

tick 2
   ↓
price = 1.0001²
```

Therefore, instead of storing every possible price directly, V3 can work with an integer **tick index**.

---

# 3. √ Why Does V3 Use the Square Root?

This is important for understanding the actual code.

V3's liquidity formulas naturally work with:

\[
\sqrt P
\]

rather than directly with `P`.

From your mathematics notes, the token amounts over a price range are:

### 📐 Formula — Token0 amount across a price range

\[
x = L\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{P_{lower}}}-\frac{1}{\sqrt{P_{upper}}}\right)
\]

### 📐 Formula — Token1 amount across a price range

\[
y = L(\sqrt{P_{upper}}-\sqrt{P_{lower}})
\]

So the protocol wants to work with:

```text
sqrt(P)
```

rather than repeatedly working with:

```text
P
```

---

# 4. 💻 Why `sqrtPriceX96`?

The EVM works with integers.

It cannot simply store:

```text
√1.0001
```

as a normal decimal number.

So Uniswap uses **fixed-point representation**.

### 📐 Formula — Q64.96 encoding

\[
sqrtPriceX96 = \sqrt P \times 2^{96}
\]

Therefore:

```text
real sqrt price
       ↓
   × 2^96
       ↓
integer stored on-chain
```

And to recover the conceptual value:

### 📐 Formula — Decode Q64.96

\[
\sqrt P = \frac{sqrtPriceX96}{2^{96}}
\]

---

# 5. 🔄 So What Does `TickMath` Actually Do?

Now everything connects:

```text
                    TICK
                      │
                      │ 1.0001^tick
                      ↓
                    PRICE
                      │
                      │ √
                      ↓
                  √PRICE
                      │
                      │ × 2^96
                      ↓
                sqrtPriceX96
```

That is basically what:

```solidity
getSqrtRatioAtTick(tick)
```

is doing.

And the reverse:

```text
              sqrtPriceX96
                    │
                    │ ÷ 2^96
                    ↓
                  √PRICE
                    │
                    │ square
                    ↓
                  PRICE
                    │
                    │ determine tick
                    ↓
                   TICK
```

is what:

```solidity
getTickAtSqrtRatio(sqrtPriceX96)
```

does.

---

# 6. 🔗 Why This Matters to the Rest of V3

You have already seen this connection elsewhere.

The overall relationship is:

```text
                 Tick
                  │
                  ↓
            TickMath
                  │
                  ↓
           sqrtPriceX96
                  │
                  ↓
           SqrtPriceMath
                  │
          ┌───────┴───────┐
          ↓               ↓
     token amounts    next price
```

And during swaps:

```text
Swap
 ↓
find next initialized tick
 ↓
TickMath
 ↓
convert tick → sqrtPriceX96
 ↓
SqrtPriceMath
 ↓
calculate token movement / next price
```

That is why `TickMath` sits underneath so much of V3's machinery.
