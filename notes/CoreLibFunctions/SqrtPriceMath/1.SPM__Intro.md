# 1. What Is `SqrtPriceMath.sol`?

The library is described as:

> **Functions based on Q64.96 sqrt price and liquidity**

Its purpose is to provide the mathematical operations that use a square root price and liquidity to calculate price movement and token amount deltas.

In simple words:

> **`SqrtPriceMath` is the calculator that tells Uniswap V3 how the price moves when tokens enter or leave a liquidity range.**

It mainly answers two questions.

### Question 1 — If I put/remove this many tokens, where does the price move?

```text
Current price
      ↓
"I add 100 token0"
      ↓
SqrtPriceMath
      ↓
New sqrt price
```

The first four functions handle this family of calculations.

### Question 2 — Between these two prices, how many tokens does this liquidity represent?

```text
Price A ───────────── Price B
          Liquidity L

How much token0?
How much token1?
```

The `getAmount0Delta()` and `getAmount1Delta()` functions handle this.

---

# 2. Why Does It Use `sqrtPrice`?

V3 works with `sqrt(P)` rather than ordinary price `P` for these calculations.

It stores the sqrt price in Q64.96 form:

```text
sqrtPriceX96 = sqrt(P) × 2^96
```

This representation makes the V3 liquidity formulas practical and precise in integer arithmetic.

For example:

```text
amount1 = L × (sqrt(Pupper) - sqrt(Plower))
```

And:

```text
amount0 = L × (1 / sqrt(Plower) - 1 / sqrt(Pupper))
```

Therefore, `SqrtPriceMath` is built around the relationships between:

- sqrt price
- liquidity
- token0
- token1

---

# 3. What Does "Delta" Mean?

A **delta** simply means the change/difference between two values.

```text
Old value = 100
New value = 120

Delta = 20
```

So `amount0 delta` means:

> How much token0 corresponds to the price movement between the two prices for the specified liquidity?

And `amount1 delta` means:

> How much token1 corresponds to that same price movement for the specified liquidity?

# 5. The Two Major Jobs of `SqrtPriceMath`

## Part A — Calculate the Next Price

These four functions:

```solidity
getNextSqrtPriceFromAmount0RoundingUp()
getNextSqrtPriceFromAmount1RoundingDown()

getNextSqrtPriceFromInput()
getNextSqrtPriceFromOutput()
```

Their job is:

> **Given the current sqrt price, liquidity, and token movement, what should the new sqrt price be?**

## Part B — Calculate Token Amounts

These functions:

```solidity
getAmount0Delta()
getAmount1Delta()

getAmount0Delta(..., int128 liquidity)
getAmount1Delta(..., int128 liquidity)
```

Their job is:

> **Given two prices and some liquidity, how much token0/token1 corresponds to that price range?**

The signed versions matter because V3's `liquidityNet` can be positive or negative when initialized ticks are crossed.

# 7. Why Rounding Matters

Throughout this library we repeatedly see:

```text
round up
round down
```

This is deliberate.

The calculations must avoid integer rounding causing the protocol to:

- give the trader too much output, or
- require too little input.

A useful question throughout this file is:

> **Who is being protected by this rounding direction?**

This is one of the most important ideas in understanding `SqrtPriceMath`.
